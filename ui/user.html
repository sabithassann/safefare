Government Fare Guidelines & Passenger Rights

Summary of key BRTA transport regulations to protect commuters from fare extortion.

🛡️

Statutory Minimum Fare

For Dhaka city buses, the official minimum fare is fixed at ৳ 10.00. No bus operator is permitted to charge more than the published distance rate for intermediate stops.

🎓

Student 50% Half-Pass

Students with valid institutional ID cards are legally entitled to 50% discount on the gazetted fare between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM on working days.

📑

Chart Display Mandate

Every public transport vehicle must prominently display the official government fare chart inside the bus cabin for passenger inspection.

⚖️

No Arbitrary Overcharging

Bus conductors are strictly prohibited from demanding arbitrary "waybill" fares not backed by official BRTA kilometer matrix charts.
